Privacy Policy

Lantern Logic · Vismind Studio · Effective date: November 17, 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Lantern Logic (“the game”) collects, why, and the choices you have. The game is developed by Vismind Studio, an independent developer (“we”, “us”). If you have any questions, contact us at vismindstudio@gmail.com.

Summary

  • There are no accounts. We never ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • Your game progress is stored only on your device.
  • The game shows ads (Google AdMob) and uses Google Firebase to understand how the game is played and to fix crashes. These Google services collect some device and usage data, described below.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress (levels completed, best times, whether you finished the tutorial) in the app's private storage on your device. We do not receive this data. If device backup is turned on (Android backup with your Google account, or iCloud or computer backup on iPhone), the save may be included in that backup, which is handled by Google or Apple under your account settings.

Information collected by third-party services

Google Analytics for Firebase

Used to understand how players use the game, for example which levels are started and completed, how often hints are used and where the tutorial is left. It collects app usage events, an app-instance identifier, the advertising ID where permitted (on iPhone only if you allow tracking), device and operating system information, language, and an approximate location derived from your IP address (Google does not store the full IP address for this purpose). Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months.

Firebase Crashlytics

Used to detect and fix crashes. When the game crashes it sends a crash report: stack trace, app state at the time of the crash, device model, operating system version and a Crashlytics installation identifier. Crash reports are kept for 90 days.

Google AdMob

Used to show ads, including optional rewarded videos that unlock extra hints and ads between levels. AdMob collects device identifiers (on Android the advertising ID and app set ID; on iPhone the identifier for vendors, and the advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking), IP address (used to estimate a general location), device information, diagnostic and performance information and your interactions with ads, in order to deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud.

Learn more about how Google uses this data: How Google uses information from apps that use its services, Google Privacy Policy, Firebase privacy information.

Consent and your choices

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: on first launch the game shows Google's consent form, where you choose whether your data may be used for personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time with Privacy settings on the game's main screen.
  • iPhone tracking permission: the game asks once whether it may track your activity across other companies' apps and websites (App Tracking Transparency). If you choose “Ask App Not to Track”, the advertising identifier is not used and ads are not personalized with it; the game works the same either way. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Personalized ads everywhere: on Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization, in your device settings (on most devices: Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads). On iPhone you can turn off personalized ads from Apple in Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising.
  • Stopping all collection: uninstalling the game stops all further data collection by the game.

How information is shared

Information is shared only with the Google services listed above, which process it to provide analytics, crash reporting and advertising. Advertising data may be shared by Google with advertising partners as described in Google's policies. We do not sell personal information and do not share it with anyone else, except where required by law.

Security

All data sent by the game to these services is encrypted in transit using TLS (HTTPS).

Children

The game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will work with Google to address it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/UK or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data. Because the game has no accounts, we cannot link the data described above to you personally; the most effective controls are the consent and advertising ID settings described above. You can also contact us with any request, and you may lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game changes. The new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
